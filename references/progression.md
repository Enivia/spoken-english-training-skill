# Progression

Infer the current stage from unaided output. Treat a single strong performance as a stretch success and require repeated evidence for lasting advancement.

## Stage 1 — Say it

Train retrieval and repair.

Observable targets:

- produce 3–5 connected sentences about one real event with light scaffolding;
- connect a feeling or opinion to a reason;
- keep speaking through a missing word with a repair phrase;
- communicate the main meaning despite minor errors.

Use short frames, common verbs, coordination, and high-frequency chunks. Reduce scaffolding as soon as the learner succeeds.

## Stage 2 — Keep it going

Train interaction and transfer.

Observable targets:

- sustain at least five small-talk turns;
- use `answer + detail + return question`;
- paraphrase when the first expression is unavailable;
- reuse two learned chunks in a different topic or scene.

Use follow-up questions, natural reactions, time and reason clauses, and story sequencing. Delay non-blocking corrections until a short conversational run finishes.

## Stage 3 — Shape the message

Train nuance, organization, and register.

Observable targets:

- tell an event with setup, change, and outcome;
- express contrast, uncertainty, attitude, and hypothetical meaning precisely;
- choose wording appropriate to a friend, stranger, coworker, or formal setting;
- vary sentence structure without losing spoken clarity.

Use discourse markers, relative and adverbial clauses, conditionals, participial phrases, and precise collocations when they serve the intended meaning. Introduce inversion or marked literary structures only when their emphasis and register are useful.

## Select the next stretch

Choose one stretch at a time:

- **Fluency:** speak longer with the same language.
- **Accuracy:** fix one recurring pattern.
- **Range:** add one reusable chunk or sentence connection.
- **Interaction:** react, follow up, or return a question.
- **Register:** say the same idea to a different person.

Keep the rest of the task familiar. Move up when the learner repeatedly meets the current stage's targets in available conversation history; otherwise offer a temporary stretch task and reassess from the next unaided attempt.
